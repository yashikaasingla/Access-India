Emergency and support numbers

Free national helplines. Save them before you need them.

  • 112All-in-one emergency (police, fire, ambulance)
  • 108Ambulance and medical emergency
  • 14567Elder Line — helpline for senior citizens
  • 1800-233-5956National disability helpline (Divyangjan)
  • 1098Childline

Numbers shown are public Indian helplines. Always confirm locally in a real emergency.
